About AHA/BHA Toner

Reveal smoother, clearer, and more radiant skin with our AHA BHA Toner. Powered by 5% Glycolic Acid (AHA) and 1% Salicylic Acid (BHA), this gentle exfoliating toner helps to unclog pores and slough away dead skin cells, revealing a brighter, more balanced complexion.

With regular use, it helps:

  • ✓ Unclog pores and reduce blackheads
  • ✓ Even out skin tone
  • ✓ Smooth rough texture
  • ✓ Control excess oil
  • ✓ Prep skin for serums & moisturizers

Suitable for: All skin types except extremely sensitive skin.

Key Ingredients

5% Glycolic Acid (AHA)

Exfoliates dead skin cells & brightens complexion

1% Salicylic Acid (BHA)

Unclogs pores & controls excess oil

Niacinamide

Evens skin tone & strengthens barrier

Aloe Vera

Soothes & hydrates skin

How to Use

  1. 1
    Cleanse

    Wash your face with a gentle cleanser and pat dry.

  2. 2
    Apply Toner

    Soak a cotton pad with toner and gently swipe across face, avoiding eye area.

  3. 3
    Wait

    Let it absorb for 1-2 minutes before applying serum.

  4. 4
    Moisturize

    Follow with serum and moisturizer. Use 2-3 times a week.

Warning: Always use sunscreen during the day when using AHA/BHA products.
